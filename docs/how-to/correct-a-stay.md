# Correct a stay

When an import got something wrong, or the hotel changed your check-out time:

1. In **Current**, click the hotel's card, then **Edit** (the pencil at the right of the
   header bar). The editor opens over the trip; on a phone it slides up from the bottom, and
   swiping it down closes it without saving.
2. Change what you need. Dates are written `2026-07-10`, times `12:00`, in the hotel's
   local time: Escale keeps the hotel's time zone.
3. Click **Save**, or press Return in any field.

**Save** stays greyed out while the stay cannot be saved; the reason is shown under the
form: a missing name, a missing or invalid check-in or check-out, a check-out that is
not after the check-in, or coordinates that are incomplete or out of range.

**Cancel**, or Esc, leaves the stay as it was.

Fields you leave untouched keep what the import found, including details Escale does
not show.
