# Add or correct a coach or ferry trip

## Add one

1. Open the menu next to **Add** (on a phone, the **+** button) and choose
   **Add manually…**. After an import that found nothing in your ticket, use the
   sheet's **Add manually** button instead: the trip then keeps the file, and
   **Show Ticket** opens it.
2. Choose **Bus** or **Ferry**.
3. Fill in at least the departure's date, time and stop (or terminal) name, and the
   arrival's stop name. The arrival time is optional; when given, it must be after the
   departure. Dates are written `2027-07-08`, times `08:00`, or chosen with the calendar
   and clock buttons. Times are in your device's time zone.
4. Click **Save**.

Escale opens it in the trip it belongs to, and sends it to your calendar at the
next sync.

## Correct one

Open the trip's detail and click the pencil (**Edit**). Times are shown and entered in
each stop's local time, as the booking had them.

## What you can fill in

- **Bus**: the bus number, then for the departure and the arrival the date, time, stop
  name and platform; the seat.
- **Ferry**: for the departure and the arrival the date, time and terminal name.
- For both: each stop's address (street, postal code, city, region, country) and its
  coordinates, the booking reference and the name the booking is under.

Write the cities: Escale names a trip after where you arrive, and groups bookings
by time and by where they leave from and arrive.
