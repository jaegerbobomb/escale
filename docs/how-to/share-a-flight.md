# Share a flight

So that whoever picks you up can follow your flight live:

1. In **Current**, click the flight's card to open its detail.
2. Click **Share flight**.
   - On a phone, the system share sheet opens: pick the app and the person.
   - On a computer, the link is copied ("Link copied"): paste it where you want.

The text reads `Follow flight AF 718: https://www.flightradar24.com/AFR718`. The link
opens Flightradar24, which shows the flight while it is in the air and otherwise its next
departures. Escale sends nothing itself: the link goes only where you send it.

**Share flight** is missing when Escale cannot be sure of the flight's callsign: a
train, or an airline it does not know. It then shows no link rather than a wrong one.

Some airlines fly under callsigns that differ from the flight number (letters instead of
digits). The link then finds no flight in the air; Escale cannot know this from the
ticket.
