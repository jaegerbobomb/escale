# Reach your hotel

1. In **Current**, click the hotel's card to open its detail.
2. The detail shows the address, check-in and check-out (in the hotel's local time),
   the room, and the hotel's contact.
3. Click the **telephone** to call it, the **e-mail** to write to it, or the
   **website** to open it: Escale hands them to your phone or computer.

## Change or cancel the booking

When the confirmation you imported links to the booking (Booking.com and Airbnb
confirmations do), the detail shows **Manage booking on Booking.com** (or the site's
name). It opens the site in your browser, where you sign in as usual: Escale never
signs into those sites for you.
